# Truyền Thuyết Dễ Thương

Create a complete responsive Vietnamese storytelling website called:

“TRUYỀN THUYẾT ĐÔ THỊ”

Tagline:

“Tôi sợ ma nên không muốn bạn phải sợ.”

LANGUAGE:

The entire website must be written in natural Vietnamese.

==================================================

1. WEBSITE CONCEPT

==================================================

This is an informational and storytelling website about urban legends, mysterious folklore, strange rumors, supernatural stories, unexplained local tales, and modern legends from Vietnam and different countries around the world.

However, DO NOT design it like a conventional horror website.

The central concept is:

“A website about scary stories for people who are curious about horror but are also afraid of scary things.”

The website should transform traditionally frightening urban legends into a cute, playful, approachable storytelling experience.

The emotional idea behind the brand is represented by the slogan:

“Tôi sợ ma nên không muốn bạn phải sợ.”

The website should make visitors feel:

Curious → amused → slightly mysterious → comfortable → interested enough to keep exploring.

The stories themselves may still discuss mysterious or eerie legends, but the VISUAL EXPERIENCE must remain friendly, charming and non-disturbing.

Avoid:

- gore

- blood

- realistic corpses

- disturbing horror photography

- jumpscare aesthetics

- demonic imagery

- aggressive red/black horror themes

- generic Halloween website design

- dark cinematic horror

- modern corporate layouts

- overly clean SaaS-style interfaces

==================================================

2. BRAND PERSONALITY

==================================================

Brand personality:

- Cute

- Curious

- Playful

- Slightly weird

- Mysterious

- Friendly

- Nostalgic

- Handmade

- Humorous

- Youthful

- Cozy

- A little spooky, but never frightening

Imagine:

“A person who is scared of ghosts but still stays up at 2 AM reading urban legends.”

The website should feel like opening a strange handmade notebook filled with rumors, mysterious stories, postcards, newspaper clippings, doodles, stickers and notes collected from around the world.

==================================================

3. VISUAL DIRECTION

==================================================

Use a handmade scrapbook / illustrated notebook / vintage storytelling aesthetic.

Combine:

- scrapbook design

- cork boards

- old paper

- notebook paper

- torn paper edges

- taped notes

- push pins

- paper clips

- stamps

- handwritten annotations

- hand-drawn doodles

- vintage printed illustrations

- slightly imperfect shapes

- subtle paper grain

- pencil and ink textures

- cute cartoon characters

The website should intentionally feel handcrafted rather than digitally perfect.

Do NOT make everything perfectly aligned.

Allow:

- slightly rotated cards

- irregular paper shapes

- asymmetric decorations

- overlapping stickers

- handwritten arrows

- doodles around sections

- small visual surprises

However, maintain good UX and readable content hierarchy.

16. INTERACTIONS

==================================================

Use subtle playful micro-interactions.

Examples:

Push pins bounce subtly.

Ghost mascot moves slightly.

Hand-drawn underline appears on hover.

Buttons feel like stickers being pressed.

Random story card flips/reveals.

Trending flame wiggles subtly.

Do NOT use excessive animations.

==================================================

17. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

==================================================

The website must work on:

Desktop

Tablet

Mobile

On mobile:

- convert navigation to hamburger menu

- maintain scrapbook feeling

- stack story cards vertically

- reduce decorative objects

- keep mascot illustrations

- maintain large readable typography

- avoid horizontal scrolling

- prioritize story readability

Decorative objects may move or disappear on smaller screens.

==================================================

18. UX REQUIREMENTS

==================================================

Despite the playful visual design, maintain:

Clear navigation

Strong hierarchy

Readable Vietnamese typography

Accessible contrast

Fast loading

Logical content structure

Responsive layout

SEO-friendly semantic HTML

Proper heading hierarchy

Clickable cards

Working search

Working filters

Reusable story components

The design should NOT feel like a static Canva poster.

It must feel like a real content-driven storytelling website.

==================================================

19. FINAL CREATIVE DIRECTION

==================================================

The final website should feel like:

“A cute scrapbook made by someone who travels around the world collecting creepy rumors but is too scared to illustrate them in a genuinely scary way.”

Think:

urban legends

+

cute ghosts

+

vintage children's illustration

+

scrapbook

+

old notebook

+

corkboard

+

hand-drawn doodles

+

internet storytelling

The website should immediately communicate:

“This is about mysterious and scary stories…”

but one second later:

“…and somehow it is adorable.”

Most importantly, preserve the brand philosophy:

“Tôi sợ ma nên không muốn bạn phải sợ.”
